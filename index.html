Chapter 01 · The Road

Chicago, Nashville, London, St. Louis, New York, and back to Chicago.

Chicago · Growing up

Grew up here.

I grew up in the Chicago suburbs playing football and basketball. My brother, Connor, is two years older than me. Running and being outside is still how I clear my head. Our family has two dogs: Addie, a pitbull-boxer mix, and Harvey, a dachshund who ended up as Salt Creek Advisory's mascot.

Nashville · Vanderbilt · Class of 2025

Vanderbilt.

I graduated cum laude with a degree in Human and Organizational Development and a minor in business. The people I met there taught me as much as the classes did. I also started a nonprofit called Trash Troopers. It's what it sounds like, and I'm still proud of it.

London · Fall 2023

A semester abroad.

I spent the fall of my junior year in London and liked it more than I expected. Nobody there had known me for more than a few weeks, which pushed me to get out and meet people. I still look for reasons to go back.

St. Louis · 2024

Investment banking.

I worked in investment banking at ButcherJoseph & Co. in St. Louis. It was my first real look at how a company actually gets sold.

New York · 2025

Private equity deal origination.

After Vanderbilt I moved to New York and worked in business development and deal origination at private equity firms, including Blue Wolf Capital and Kingfish Capital. I lived on the Lower East Side and liked the city more than I expected to.

That work put me in a lot of conversations with business owners. Many had spent decades building their companies, and selling was one of the biggest decisions they would ever make. I wanted to work for those owners directly.

Chicago · Now

Back home.

So I came home to Chicago and started Salt Creek Advisory. Connor joined full-time in September 2026, and the two of us run it together. I also host two podcasts and write on Substack.

I'm working to build a real community of business owners and dealmakers here in Chicago. If you're part of it, or want to be, reach out.

Chapter 02 · Salt Creek Advisory · Chicago

We help owners sell their businesses.

Salt Creek Advisory is a family-owned, Chicago-based investment bank that I run with my brother, Connor Pitts. We only work on the sell side. That means we represent owners of family- and founder-owned businesses in the lower middle market when they decide to sell, and we never take a buyer's side.

Most owners sell a business once. Our job is to help that one sale go well. For an owner, that usually means we:

  • Help get the business ready for a sale, including the numbers and the story buyers will ask about, and less dependent on the owner.
  • Find buyers who are a real fit, such as strategic buyers, family offices, and private equity firms.
  • Run a competitive sale process, so the owner can compare more than one option.
  • Help the owner weigh offers and letters of intent on price, terms, and fit, not only the headline number.
  • Work with the owner's accountant, lawyer, and other advisors through closing.

Connor and I work every engagement personally, from the first conversation to closing. Connor joined the firm full-time in September 2026. Before that he worked on more than 40 transactions in corporate development at Cadence Education, and in investment banking at Brown Gibbons Lang & Company in Chicago.

We can't promise a price or a closing, and we won't. The firm's website explains how a Salt Creek sale process works and has guides for owners thinking about selling.

Chapter 05 · HelmIQ

Why we built HelmIQ.

Running a sale means keeping track of a lot: which buyers we've talked to, what each one said, and what needs to happen next. That information belongs to our clients and to us. We wanted to own it, not hand it to someone else's platform.

So I built HelmIQ myself, using Claude Code. We run Salt Creek's deals on it, and a few other firms license it from us. I'm not out selling it. It's mostly a tool we built for ourselves, and I still work on it every day.
