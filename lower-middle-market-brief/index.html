About the show

Lower Middle Market Brief is a short podcast about deals, investors, operators, and trends across the lower middle market. Each episode breaks down what's happening, what matters, and who's worth knowing, in about 10 to 15 minutes.

What an episode covers

Most episodes walk through the deals announced in a given week, including:

  • New private equity platform investments
  • Add-on acquisitions by sponsor-backed companies
  • Strategic acquisitions
  • Sponsor exits
  • Fund closes and fundraising

About the host

Jack Pitts is co-founder and principal of Salt Creek Advisory, a family-owned, Chicago-based investment bank he runs with his brother, Connor. The firm represents owners of family- and founder-owned businesses in the lower middle market when they sell their companies. Jack also hosts The Making Of Hosted by Jack Pitts, a long-form interview show with founders and business owners, and writes on Substack. More about Jack.

Suggest a deal, a guest, or a topic: jack@saltcreekadvisory.com.

Episodes discuss publicly announced deals for general information. Nothing on the show is investment advice or an offer to buy or sell any security. See the disclosures.
